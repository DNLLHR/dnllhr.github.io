Privacy policy for BlockSkill

Last updated: November 10, 2026

In short

BlockSkill does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking, no analytics services and no in-app purchases. The app does not connect to any server.

Controller

Daniel Lehr
Södingberg 164, 8152 Geistthal-Södingberg, Austria
Email: dnllhr@gmx.at

What is stored on your device

The app only stores locally on your device: your settings, your best scores per level and the automatically saved game (encrypted). This data never leaves your device and I have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes it too. Depending on your iOS settings it may be part of your own iCloud or device backup, which Apple manages for you.

Crash reports via Apple

If you have turned on “Share with App Developers” in iOS under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple provides me with anonymized crash and usage reports. They contain nothing that lets me identify you. I only use them to find and fix bugs. The legal basis is the consent you gave to Apple (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw there at any time.

App Store

Downloads and updates are handled by Apple's App Store, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Contact by email

If you write to me, I use your email address and message only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). I delete them once the request is settled and no legal retention period applies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from children either.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Just send me an email. If you believe the processing of your data violates data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that processes data differently, I will update this policy beforehand.
