Färbeln

The tavern card game from Styria.

Coming soon to the App Store

Färbeln is a tavern card game from Styria, Austria. Everyone holds four cards. You draw from the stock or the discard pile, swap cleverly and collect as many points as you can in one suit. Think your hand is the best? Knock: everyone else gets exactly one more turn, then the cards are revealed. The loser gives up a tally mark. You play at a wooden table against two to five regulars, with traditional German-suited or French-suited cards whose court figures were drawn especially for this app. Five short lessons at the table teach the game, and house rules let you play it the way your local tavern does.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to me, I will answer as soon as I can.

How do I learn Färbeln?

Tap „Färbeln lernen“ (learn) in the main menu. Five short lessons guide you through the game right at the table. Each one earns a stamp, and all five stamps unlock the master card back. The full rules are in the main menu under „Regeln“.

How do I switch between German-suited and French-suited cards?

In Settings („Einstellungen“) under „Karten und Klang“ › „Kartenblatt“. That is also where you turn on the master card back once you have unlocked it.

How do I turn off game sounds or the tavern ambience?

In Settings under „Karten und Klang“. Game sounds and tavern ambience can be switched off separately.

We play it differently. Can I change the rules?

Yes. Under Settings › „Hausregeln“ (house rules) you can choose sevens as jokers, a minimum score for knocking, or whether knocking is allowed in the first round. „Wertung“ sets tally marks and tie-breaks. „Standardregeln wiederherstellen“ resets everything.

My game was interrupted. Is it lost?

No. A game in progress is saved on your device. Continue it from the main menu with „Partie fortsetzen“.

Is there any money involved?

No. Färbeln is played purely for fun. You only play for tally marks, never for money.

How do I report a bug?

Send me an email with your device, iOS version and app version. The app version is shown in Settings under „Rechtliches & Info“. A screenshot helps a lot.
