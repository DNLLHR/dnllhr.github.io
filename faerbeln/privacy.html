Privacy policy for Färbeln

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short

Färbeln runs entirely offline on your device. The app collects no personal data and sends no data to any server. There is no user account, no advertising, no tracking or analytics software and no in-app purchases.

Controller

Daniel Lehr
Södingberg 164, 8152 Geistthal-Södingberg, Austria
Email: dnllhr@gmx.at

What is stored on your device

The app only stores the following locally on your device: your settings, a game in progress and your progress in the lessons. This data never leaves your device and I have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes it too. Depending on your iOS settings, it may be included in your own iCloud or device backup, which Apple manages for you.

Crash reports via Apple

If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in iOS under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple provides me with anonymised crash and usage reports. They contain nothing that lets me identify you. I use them only to find and fix bugs. The legal basis is your consent given to Apple (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw there at any time.

App Store

Downloading and updating the app is handled by Apple’s App Store, which is covered by Apple’s privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Contact by email

If you write to me, I use your email address and message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). I delete them once the request is settled, unless I am legally required to keep them.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children either.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Just send me an email. If you believe your data is processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects how data is handled, I will update this policy beforehand.
